Consulting

My consulting focuses on the economics of information technology, industries related to information technology, and issues related to the economics of consumer behavior or product demand. The types of cases I have worked on in the past include:

Patent Damages
Assessment in patent litigation for operating systems software, e-commerce systems, network hardware, microprocessors, memory devices, communications equipment, mobile devices and embedded systems. (Representative cases: Stragent v. Intel, WARF v. Apple, VLSI v. Intel)
Consumer Damages
From fraud, false advertising, data breach, privacy violations, or product misrepresentation in laptop computers, software, and online services as well as other consumer products outside the IT industry such as automobiles, trucks, all-terrain vehicles, pet food, appliances and beauty products. Many of these are consumer class actions. (Representative cases: Kelley et al. v. Microsoft, Callaway et al. v. Mercedes-Benz, Nemet et al. v. Volkswagen)
Antitrust Issues
In software and consumer electronics. (Representative cases: In re TFT-LCD (Flat Panel) Antitrust Litigation, Epic v. Apple)
IT Industry Practices
Relating to enterprise software, security software, database software, media creation and development tools. (Dolby v. Adobe)
IT Project Problems
In a number of industries including data providers, grocery, hotels, government services and customer support providers.
